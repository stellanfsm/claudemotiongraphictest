# Research brief: short vertical ads for Seventh Seal

These are the research notes behind the two reels [`googlet/`](../googlet/) and [`sjekk/`](../sjekk/). They draw on 40+ searches, mostly primary sources: Meta, TikTok, Google, Eurostat, SSB, Forbrukerrådet and peer-reviewed papers. **[shaky]** marks US-only surveys, platforms grading their own ads, and findings that have not replicated well.

## Retention and format

- **The first seconds carry the ad.**
  - Meta/Nielsen: up to 47 % of a video campaign's value comes in the first 3 s, and up to 74 % in the first 10 s ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/updated-features-for-video-ads)).
  - TikTok: the first 2–2.5 s drive recall and awareness. Over 63 % of the highest-CTR ads state the key message in the first 3 s ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)).
  - Lumen for TikTok: a CTA in the opening frame gave +44 % conversion ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/Power_Creative_Elements/pc/en)).
- **Length.**
  - TikTok: any length can work, but the first 6 s are vital ([SMB playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf)).
  - Google: 10–30 s for action-oriented Shorts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en)).
  - The two reels run 34 s, and both work as an ad within their first 6 s.
- **Sound off vs. on.**
  - 69 % watch with sound off in public (Verizon/Publicis, US 2019) **[US]**.
  - Captions add +12 % view time on Meta.
  - Custom music gives +78 % aided recall over recognisable tracks (TikTok).
  - Rule: make it fully understandable muted, and give it an original sound.
- **Safe zones (1080×1920).**
  - Meta Reels: keep the top 14 %, the bottom 35 % and 6 % on each side clear ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-reels)).
  - TikTok: about 130 px at the top, 140 px on the right and 370–484 px at the bottom.
  - The intersection is roughly **y 280–1240**.
- **Native beats polished.**
  - Native TikTok creative gives +19 % ad recall (Kantar).
  - Captions give +58 % recall, and a title at the start +48 % brand recall.
  - Ads following Google's ABCD (brand early and often) gave up to +30 % short-term sales likelihood (Kantar, 11,000 ads).
- **Looping.** Replays count as views on Shorts (since 2025). Make the end card loop into the first frame.

## Persuasion

- **Loss aversion** ([Tversky & Kahneman 1992](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00122574); smaller than once thought, per Gal & Rucker 2018). Frame what is lost: "they call the next one".
- **Zero price** ([Shampanier, Mazar & Ariely 2007](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.1060.0254)) and **risk reversal**. The free first draft and the free website check.
- **Anchoring** ([Tversky & Kahneman 1974](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124)). Show the market range first. A Norwegian price guide lists 17 000–50 000 kr for a custom site ([Byråmatch](https://www.xn--byrmatch-c0a.no/fagbloggen/hva-koster-en-nettside-i-norge)).
- **Specificity** ([Schindler & Yalch 2006](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279544736_It_Seems_Factual_But_Is_It_Effects_of_Using_Sharp_versus_Round_Numbers_in_Advertising_Claims)). "3 revisjonsrunder", "2–4 uker", "3–5 punkter".
- **Reciprocity**, **unity** and **commitment** (Cialdini). A free gift, "two of us in Oslo", and a small first step.
- **Unfinished tasks pull** (the Ovsiankina effect; the Zeigarnik memory effect did not replicate, per [Ghibellini & Meier 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w)). Two findings left blurred: "+2 til".
- **The peak-end rule** ([Kahneman et al. 1993](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1993.tb00589.x)). The reveal sits at about 70 % of the runtime, and the end is calm and confident.
- **Mere exposure** ([Zajonc 1968](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0025848)). The logo bug is on screen from the first second, and the same palette and sound appear in every reel.
- **One CTA, with the effort stated** (choice overload is strongest for high-stakes, non-expert choices).
- **Reading load.** About 3 words/s (BBC subtitle norms) and at most ~7 words per card, in big type.
- **Social proof must be real.** Fake reviews and invented customers are illegal under markedsføringsloven ([Forbrukertilsynet](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/lov-og-rett/veiledninger-og-retningslinjer/someveiledning)).

## Mistakes the reels avoid

- A logo intro, or a slow setup.
- Text in the bottom 35 %.
- More than one CTA.
- Jargon (SEO, CMS, responsive).
- Fake reviews or customers; example sites are labelled «Eksempel».
- Promises like "guaranteed more customers".
- A "fra" price without the monthly fee.
- Over-polished agency gloss.
- Music that carries the message.
- Cards of more than ~7 words.
- An ending without the URL on screen.
